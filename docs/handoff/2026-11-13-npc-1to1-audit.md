## Rule for this work

BECOME NPC must reproduce stock ped/cop behaviour exactly. Anything the mod
added that a stock ped or cop would not do is a bug, not a feature. The only
allowed divergences are the ones the player-ped needs because it has no AI
brain: input stand-down, wander drive, combat hand-off, stat/fear/created-by
borrow, ID-check hold and the cop patrol dispatch.

## Removed in 475

`VCModNpcCopWeaponRange` and its `KillCharOnFootArmed` hook. It shortened the
armed cop's stop range (`range / 3`), which is not stock: a stock cop chasing
an NPC criminal uses the armed objective with the weapon's **full** range.
CopAI's close-in pursuit is hard-coded to the player and never runs against an
NPC, so "runs in like a stock cop" was a wrong target — stock cops stand at
range against NPC criminals too.

## Open 1:1 divergences, in order

### 1. A blocking car (tried in 476, reverted in 478)

Stock does **not** play the tired pose at a car. For a stopped/slow car a
pedestrian calls `SetDirectionToWalkAroundVehicle` and walks around it; the
player branch only looks at the car (`AnnoyPlayerPed`/shake-fist). The tired
pose is the blocked-ped panic counter for walls/objects.

Build 476 let NPC mode take the pedestrian branch (`Ped.cpp:2789`). In play it
froze him: `SetDirectionToWalkAroundVehicle` sets `PED_FOLLOW_PATH`, and a
stock AI ped resumes its objective after the detour; the player ped has no
objective layer to re-take the body, so he stood in place, did not chase and
did not resume after an ID check. Reverted in 478.

If this is retried, it needs an NPC-side recovery for `PED_FOLLOW_PATH`
(restore the objective/wander when the detour path ends), not just the branch
swap. Until then the player branch is the stock behaviour for a player ped.

### 2. Wandering off the path

`m_distanceToCountSeekDone` already defaults to 1.0 (`Ped.cpp:139`) and the
player-NPC override at `WanderPath` sets the same 1.0, so the radius is not a
divergence. Compare next: the player override's PED_SEEK_ENTITY/PED_SEEK_POS
handling, `m_vecSeekPos` updates and the stroll gait against a stock ped's
`Seek()` path; the off-path moment is visual, so the wander diag
(`VCMOD wander: state=… path=… speed=…`) plus a period recording is the
evidence.
