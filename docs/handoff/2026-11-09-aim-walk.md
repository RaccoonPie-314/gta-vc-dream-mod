# AIM WALK — a lock-on no longer roots the player

Date: 2026-11-09
Repo/branch/HEAD: `reVC/`, `vcmods`, `d6070c5` — committed and published in
`gta-vc-dream-mod` as `db403d1`. Confirmed in play by the owner.
Installed: `~/Games/VC/reVC.app` is `vcmods build 165` (installed by this session;
no game session was running, `pgrep` checked first).

Verified in play. The walk and the recovery both behave as written below.

## The question, answered

A locked-on target used to root the player for every gun except Colt45, Tec9 and
Silenced Ingram. While a lock is held the camera leaves the mouse cam
(`MODE_SYPHON`), so movement runs through `PlayerControlZelda`, and
`CPlayerPed::MovementDisabledBecauseOfTargeting` zeroed the stick there for every
weapon without `WEAPONFLAG_CANAIM_WITHARM`. `weapon.dat` sets that flag on the
three pistols only, so the arm IK let those walk and nothing else could. The
recovery/lowered hold was not the cause; it was just the state he was stuck in.

## Change

One row, **AIM WALK** (`bVCModAimWalk`, ini `AimWalk`), default on, in
ALL ON/OFF on the COMBAT page after GUN RECOVERY.

- `reVC/src/extras/vcmodpanel.{h,cpp}`, `src/core/re3.cpp`: the switch, the row,
  the debug-menu entry, the ini read, the ALL ON/OFF list.
- `reVC/src/peds/PedFight.cpp`:
  - `VCModAimHoldMoving(ped)` is now the one moving rule (walk/run move state or
    `m_fMoveSpeed > 0.1`); the two-hand hold's inline copy is gone.
  - `VCModAimWalkWeapon(ped, info)`: the row on, the player, standing (not
    ducking), not the two-hand colt, and a weapon without `CANAIM_WITHARM`.
  - `PointGunAt`: while that predicate holds and he is moving, the parked aim
    clip fades out (`blendDelta -8`, `ASSOC_DELETEFADEDOUT` cleared so the clip
    stays alive) and the function returns before it re-parks — locomotion owns
    the body. That branch stands down while a recovery tail is still running, so
    the tail is never cut. The last block fades the aim clip back in (`+8`) once
    he stands still, and the parked lowered hold a walk faded comes back in the
    same way.
  - `VCModAimTailRunning(ped)`: the tail flag *and* its clip still playing. The
    parked hold is not a tail, so walking from the hold stays free.
- `reVC/src/peds/PlayerPed.cpp`:
  - `MovementDisabledBecauseOfTargeting`: with the row on it returns
    `m_pPointGunAt && (m_nPedState == PED_ATTACK || VCModAimTailRunning(this))` —
    the shot and the lowering tail plant the player, everything after them moves.
    The two-hand colt's own freeze (shot and tail) is checked first and is
    unchanged.
  - `RotatePlayerToTrackTarget`: stands down while he walks, or the aim turn pins
    the facing and every direction key becomes "forward" (the ninth-pass bug).
  - `ProcessPlayerWeapon`: `bCanPointGunAtTarget` no longer drops the lock while
    he walks — the gun is down, so a torso that cannot reach the target is not a
    reason to lose it.
- `reVC/utils/test_vc_aimwalk.py`: 8 tests — the two weapon/moving predicates
  compiled with stubs (row, player, ducking, two-hand pose, arm-aim weapon,
  walk/run/speed cut) plus wiring checks (the shot-and-tail gate, the tail helper,
  the facing stand-down, the lock guard, the fade out/in including the hold coming
  back, the colt hold using the same moving rule, row and ini).
  `test_vc_cop_pistol.py`'s `test_moving_lets_the_body_animate` now asserts the
  shared helper instead of the inline copy; `test_vc_passenger.py`'s row-count
  fixture is 36 (twelve combat rows).

Pistols (`CANAIM_WITHARM`) and the two-hand colt behave exactly as before.
Crouch-aim keeps the stock freeze on purpose.

## In play, to check

1. Lock on with the Uzi, rifle, shotgun or M60: walking works at once, the weapon
   rides in the carry with normal walk/run legs, and the target stays locked.
2. Stop: the weapon comes back up and points at the target within a moment.
3. Shoot, then push a direction: the recovery tail plays the lowering out **first**
   while he stays planted, then he walks. This is the rule the owner asked for
   after playing build 163, whose walk cut the tail and dropped the gun at once.
4. Fire from the walk: the burst plants him, the tail lowers, then the walk takes
   over; the lowered hold comes back when he stops.
5. Standing after a burst: the lowered hold stays (GUN RECOVERY unchanged).
6. Pistols and the two-hand colt: unchanged, including the colt shot stance and
   its locked-on movement.
7. Turn AIM WALK off: every non-pistol gun roots him on a lock like stock.

If the walk looks like it glides under a still-raised weapon, the suspect is the
fade never reaching zero — check `VCModAimHoldMoving` sees the move state on the
first frame after the stick goes down.
