- **Rappel sway fix — pin the hull basis** (uncommitted, built, harness green, not seen in game): the second in-game run still had the men swaying on the way down even though the hull was held at a fixed station. Cause: `VCModChaseUpdate` runs after `CWorld::Process`, so the per-frame `SetPosition` was the only thing pinned; the autopilot (`CCarCtrl::SteerAIHeliTowardsTargetCoors`, `src/control/CarCtrl.cpp:2646`) had already rebuilt the hull's basis from the residual move speed — `up = normalise(3*speed.x, 3*speed.y, 1)` — so the hull kept rocking, and its heading kept turning toward the station. The rope tops are hull-space points (`VCModChaseRopeTop` = `GetMatrix() * (slot ±2.4, -1, -1.5)`), so the hull's tilt/yaw dragged the whole rope line sideways. `VCModChaseCaptureHeliHeading` now records an upright forward/right the first frame the hull is on station (`bVCModChaseHeliHeld`), and `VCModChasePinHeli` restores position, zero speeds and that basis (`GetRight/GetForward/GetUp` + `UpdateRW`) in `HOLD` and every `DROP` tick, after the autopilot has run. The descent now also matches the stock numbers: rate 0.003/frame and release 2 m above ground (was 0.0035 and 1.3).
- **How the stock SWAT descent works** (`CHeli::SendDownSwat`, `Heli.cpp:722`; `CHeli::Process` rope loop, `Heli.cpp:533`; `CCopPed::ProcessHeliSwat`, `CopPed.cpp:811`): the heli spawns each man at a hull-space anchor (`FindSwatPositionRelativeToHeli`, ±1.2 lateral), `bUsesCollision=false`, `m_pRopeEntity = heli`, and blends `ANIM_STD_ABSEIL`; each frame it re-registers the rope at the same anchor so the rope top follows the hull exactly. The ped's own process advances `m_fAbseilPos += 0.003*dt`, sets `m_vecMoveSpeed.z = -0.03`, gives each man a tiny random yaw, samples `CRopes::FindCoorsAlongRope(id, t)` and teleports there; when he is within 2 m of the ground it snaps to `PED_IDLE`, zeroes speeds, `SetInTheAir()` + `bKnockedUpIntoAir`. Smoothness there is the constant rope parameter, the hull-anchored top and the natural-speed clip — not collision physics. Our rappel keeps the constant rate and the same release, but uses the analytic straight line under the pinned hull instead of the simulated chain, exactly as the climb does.

## Engine traps already paid for
- A driving ped that dies is destroyed by the engine in the same frame: keep the leader and crew by pool handle, never raw pointers.
- Path nodes have no usable reverse link and lane links are unreliable: route by distance sampling from the station.
- Police vehicles spawn `CARLOCK_LOCKED_INITIALLY`: set `CARLOCK_UNLOCKED` on every mission car.
- `CCopPed::CopAI` is wanted-driven and ignores `bIsPlayerFriend`: mute the player's wanted record instead.
- `m_nNumMaxPassengers` excludes the driver; total seats = that plus one.
- An AI heli must stay `STATUS_PHYSICS`: `CCarAI`/`CCarCtrl::SteerAICarWithPhysics` only runs the mission for `STATUS_PHYSICS`/`STATUS_SIMPLE`, and `FlyingControl` returns right after its damping for any non-player status, so nothing fights `MISSION_HELI_FLYTOCOORS`. `CPed::WarpPedIntoCar` sets the car to `STATUS_PLAYER` when the ped is the player, so re-set `STATUS_PHYSICS` after seating him as a passenger.
- `CRopes::FindCoorsAlongRope` interpolates the node array, so the rope's *shape* is the climb path; the pool now hangs a new rope straight down from the anchor instead of bunching every segment there.
- Pinning an AI heli means pinning its **basis**, not just its position: `SteerAIHeliTowardsTargetCoors` rebuilds `right/forward/up` from `up = normalise(3*speed.x, 3*speed.y, 1)` and keeps turning `m_fOrientation` every frame inside `CWorld::Process`, which runs *before* `VCModChaseUpdate`; the hull therefore rocks and yaws unless the basis is restored after it. A "held" heli that only gets `SetPosition` still drags anything attached in hull space.
- Jumping a ped out of a car by hand needs the seat slot, `m_pMyVehicle`, `m_carInObjective`, `bInVehicle` and the objective all cleared; a leftover `ENTER_CAR_AS_PASSENGER` makes the boarding AI walk the man straight back.
- The car heli's flight cannot look like the stock chopper through `TellHeliToGoToCoors`: `SteerAIHeliTowardsTargetCoors` (CarCtrl.cpp:2646) is a reach-a-point model with a 0.2× speed ramp inside 10 m and a `±0.03` turn rate, so a station refreshed every half second reads as hover/step, not pursuit. Port `CHeli::Process` instead and drive the hull from the mod tick (after `CWorld::Process`, like the pin). Two details make that safe: the mission must stay `MISSION_HELI_FLYTOCOORS` because that check is what sets `skipPhysics` (`Automobile.cpp:692`), and the model's own speed cannot live in `m_vecMoveSpeed` because the autopilot rewrites it before the tick. `m_fOrientation` is the CHeli heading *plus* pi — CHeli's heading runs against its forward vector (`Heli.cpp:432`, "not really forward"), while `SteerAIHeliTowardsTargetCoors` builds `forward = (cos, sin, 0)` from `m_fOrientation`; writing the bare CHeli angle flips the hull 180° when `HOLD` gives it back to the autopilot.
- Both helis fire the same round: `FireOneInstantHitRound` (Weapon.cpp:3022) is the *only* heli gun — flash, `PARTICLE_HELI_ATTACK` tracer (0.15× the shot vector, 500 ms); the stock chopper's "bigger" trace is just a longer shot. Targeting lives entirely in the caller.
- **A `CAutomobile` on `MISSION_HELI_FLYTOCOORS` still collides with the world.** `skipPhysics` only suppresses the physics integration inside `CAutomobile::ProcessControl`; that same block sets `bIsInSafePosition = false`, so `CWorld::Process` runs `ProcessCollision()` on the hull, resolves it against buildings and damages it. The AI `CHeli` never does: it overrides `ProcessControl` and ends with `RemoveAndAdd(); bIsInSafePosition = true;` (`Heli.cpp:548`). Set `bUsesCollision = false` on a car heli that must survive (mission transport); that also removes it from `ProcessLineOfSight`, which matters because shots and lock checks start at the hull centre and would otherwise hit the hull itself.
- `CPopulation::AddPed(PEDTYPE_COP, x, ...)` reads `x` as an **eCopType (0-5), not a model index** (`Population.cpp:456` → `CCopPed((eCopType)x)`). Passing a model index leaves a vice ped (MI_VICE1 = 97) with `m_modelIndex = -1` because no cop-type case matches and `SetModelIndex` is never called; `CWorld::Add` then crashes in `GetBoundCentre` reading the col model of model -1. It also silently swaps street→FBI and FBI→SWAT. Use `VCModChaseCopType(unit)` and put the vice variant in the `modifier` argument.
- **Do not build a `CAutomobile` on `MI_CHOPPER`** — it is the AI `CHeli` model (IDE type "heli"), and two separate things break. (a) `CHeli::InitHelis` (`Heli.cpp:759`) points its col model at the shared ped model `CTempColModels::ms_colModelPed1`; the `CAutomobile` constructor (`Automobile.cpp:200`) bolts 4 suspension lines onto whatever col model the vehicle uses, mutating the col model **every ped shares**, and the next ped collision calls `ProcessLineTriangle` with the `nil` linedists `CPed::ProcessEntityCollision` passes (`Ped.cpp:3063`) and crashes at 0x4. (b) `PreprocessHierarchy` uses `heliIds`, so its `HELI_CHASSIS` node takes hierarchy id 1 — the same as `CAR_WHEEL_RF` — and the constructor's `IsRealHeli` wheel-hiding (`Automobile.cpp:234`) switches the body off, so it spawns **invisible**. The rideable police heli is `MI_POLMAV` (IDE type "car" + `HANDLING_IS_HELI`, a "RealHeli"): car node ids, a proper vehicle col model, native 3 passenger seats.
- `VCModChaseTickSquadDismount` gates on `pVCModChaseSquadCar`, so an air start (no transport) never re-issues the squad's fight order; keep squad objectives alive from `VCModChaseCorner` for the no-car case.
- `CPed::WarpPedIntoCar` picks the seat from the objective it is called with (`Ped.cpp:9378`): `ENTER_CAR_AS_DRIVER` calls `SetDriver`, `ENTER_CAR_AS_PASSENGER` takes the first free `pPassengers` slot, and any other objective returns without seating. So the first man warped into an arrival car must be given the driver objective and the rest the passenger one; a passenger objective on the first warp leaves `pDriver` nil and the `AutoPilot` mission has nothing to drive.
- Never hand a spawned cop a weapon: `CCopPed`'s constructor has already armed it by type, and `GiveWeapon` replaces that slot, which is how the Army tier ended up with a UZI. `GiveDelayedWeapon` also requests its own model; `GiveWeapon` does not, so a weapon given by the mission needs its model requested separately.
