## What the feature is

Three Vercetti gang members (`PEDTYPE_GANG7`, `PGA`/`PGB`, M4 each) in a
four-door car. `V` calls or dismisses them, `P` boards any car as a passenger.
The car arrives with a crew driver; `F` on the crew car takes the wheel without
a carjack (the driver steps out, then takes the back seat), `P` rides as a
passenger. When Tommy leaves the car they follow on foot with the stock leader
AI and fight with him. Dismissal or death hands leadership to one of them and
lets them live on as a street gang.

Settled details worth not re-litigating:

- The stock entourage already exists: `CPed::SetLeader` + `UpdateFromLeader`
  (`src/peds/PedAI.cpp:598`). No new AI.
- The car has no stock Vercetti assignment (`src/peds/Gangs.cpp:38`); the
  four-door shelf defaults to the Admiral.
- `F` is stock everywhere except the crew car and the summoned limo.
- `P` works with or without a driver; a driverless crew car gets a man at the
  wheel from the fill pass.
- Dismissal uses `RANDOM_CHAR` so the world may reclaim the group only when it
  is far away and off-screen. The mod never despawns a living dismissed man,
  near or far. Dead members are removed.
- Death is hooked in `CPed::SetDie` (`src/peds/Ped.cpp:7055`) so the dead-leader
  flee (`PedAI.cpp:631`) never runs.
- `PASSENGER MODE` is retired; the key replaces it.
- Mechanism correction found during design: `UpdateFromLeader`'s leave/board
  block only runs when `IsPedInControl()`, which is false for `PED_DRIVING` and
  `PED_PASSENGER`; in-vehicle crew are immune. The only place links are lifted is
  while men walk to a car Tommy rides as a passenger.
- Known limit: the save writer stores position/health/weapons, not the leader
  link, so a save taken after a handover reloads them leaderless. Out of scope.
