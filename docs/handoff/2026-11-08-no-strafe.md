# No strafe — the keys choose the heading

Date: 2026-11-08
Repo/branch/HEAD: `reVC/`, `vcmods`, `d01bb3c` plus uncommitted batches 1–5 and this change.
Installed: `~/Games/VC/reVC.app`, `vcmods build 99`, installed with no session running.

A panel switch, `NO STRAFE`, turns off the mouse-cam side-step: holding W/A/S/D
picks a direction on the screen, the body turns into it and walks forward, the way
a ped does. Aiming or firing hands the heading back to the mouse camera, so the
torso still follows the crosshair and the stock strafe stance still works while
shooting.

## What ships

1. **`src/extras/vcmodpanel.{h,cpp}`**
   - `bVCModNoStrafe` (default off), panel row `NO STRAFE` after `WALK MODE`,
     debug-menu tweak, left out of `ALL ON/OFF` like `WALK MODE`.
   - `VCModPlayerTurnsWithInput()`: true when the flag is on and the player is not
     aiming or firing (`pad->GetTarget()`, `pad->GetWeapon()`, `PED_ATTACK`,
     `PED_AIM_GUN` all hand the heading back).
   - ini key `[VC Mods] NoStrafe`; like every other switch there is no writer, so
     the panel flip lives for the session and the ini only seeds the default.

2. **`src/peds/PlayerPed.cpp`**
   - `PlayerControl1stPersonRunAround`: the destination heading is
     `GetRadianAngleBetweenPoints(0,0,-leftRight,upDown) - TheCamera.Orientation`
     — the same sum `PlayerControlZelda` already uses — instead of
     `TheCamera.Orientation`.
   - `ProcessAnimGroups`: both stock side-step conditions also require
     `!VCModPlayerTurnsWithInput()`, so an NPC body keeps its own walk clips.

3. **`src/peds/Ped.cpp`** — `CalculateNewVelocity` takes the early return in this
   mode and zeroes `m_fWalkAngle`. This is the part that matters: without it,
   `WorkOutHeadingForMovingFirstPerson` adds the pad angle to a body that is no
   longer facing the camera, and the heading feeds back into itself (the ped
   circles). Movement comes from the walk clip's root motion, exactly the NPC
   path.

4. **`src/core/Cam.cpp`** — `Process_FollowPedWithMouse` no longer writes the
   ped's heading from `Front.Heading()` while the scheme is active. The camera
   runs after the world, so this write was the last word each frame and would
   have undone the movement change on its own. Nothing else in the camera
   changed: Beta stays mouse-only on foot, no auto-centring, look-behind still
   works.

5. **Harness** — `utils/test_vc_moveanim.py` gained `NoStrafeTests`: wiring
   checks (flag, panel row, ini, heading line, early return, both anim gates,
   camera gate) and a numeric check that W/A/S/D land on the pressed screen
   direction for five camera headings. `test_vc_passenger.py` row count 35 → 36.
   All 18 `utils/test_vc_*.py` harnesses pass.

## What to look at in play

- Walk and run with the camera pointed at the flank: A/D should turn him and run
  forward, not slide sideways.
- Diagonals are one 45° heading, not a slide.
- S turns him around and runs toward the camera; there is no backpedal (ped
  groups have no backpedal clip).
- Holding aim or fire puts him back on the crosshair with the stock strafe.
- Wearing an NPC body, the side-step clips should be gone; the body's own walk
  clips play in every direction.
- Jump still steers; turning while walking should arc, not snap.

Not played yet. Turn rate comes from the ped stat (`m_headingRate`, 15 per 50 ms
tick for the player in `pedstats.dat` — the same rate the peds turn at) — if 90°
turns feel sluggish, that is the knob, not the camera.
